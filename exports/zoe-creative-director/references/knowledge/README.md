# Knowledge — creative-director (Zoe)

Documents in this folder are read by **creative-director** before every answer. Put the consultant's own facts here: they take priority over the general research in `references/research/`.

## What belongs here
- Creative briefs and scripts
- Winning/losing ad notes
- Creator roster and usage rights
- Brand assets for creatives

## What does not belong here
- Passwords, API keys, tokens or other credentials
- Customer personal data (names, emails, addresses); summarize or anonymize instead
- General market research that every agent needs: put it in `references/research/`

## Conventions
- Prefer Markdown, CSV or PDF. Name files `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.ext` so the newest is obvious.
- Put a one-line summary and the source/date at the top of each Markdown file.
- Remove or archive outdated files (`archive/` subfolder): the agent reads everything here.
