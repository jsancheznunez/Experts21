# 03 — Target Customers

> Research date: November 2026. Survey data comes from eMarketer, DHL, CivicScience, Sprout Social and Salesforce; methods differ, so figures are indicative.

## Who buys from dropshipping-style stores

Dropshipping stores sell mostly through **paid social discovery** (people who weren't searching for the product). The customers who buy this way are:

### Primary: Millennials (≈30–45)
- Highest purchasing power among social shoppers; the most active buyers on Facebook (~68% have purchased there, DHL 2026).
- eMarketer: 43% of millennials bought through an in-app social shop in the past three months, on par with Gen Z, and they are TikTok Shop's best spenders.
- Often parents and pet owners, buying for the home. Good fit for pet, home and wellness niches.
- Channel: **Meta (Facebook + Instagram)** first, TikTok second.

### Secondary: Gen Z (≈18–29)
- Discover products on TikTok and Instagram; 49% use TikTok to find their next purchase (Sprout Social 2026).
- 56% of US Gen Z have bought something because of an influencer (CivicScience).
- 68% are at least somewhat likely to buy on TikTok Shop from a brand they already use.
- Lower average order value and more price-sensitive, and the most likely to compare prices with Temu/Shein.
- Channel: **TikTok, Instagram Reels, creators/UGC**.

### Tertiary: Gen X / Boomers (45+)
- Smaller share of social purchases but higher trust once converted and fewer price comparisons. Respond to Facebook feed ads and email.
- Good for comfort, wellness, home and gift products.

## Customer personas (starting set)

| Persona | Profile | Motivation | Objections to answer |

| **"Pet parent Priya"** | 28–42, urban/suburban, 1–2 dogs/cats | Pet wellbeing, cute content, gifting | Quality, safety, shipping time |

| **"Wellness Wendy"** | 25–45, mostly female | Sleep, stress, self-care | Proof (reviews, results), honest claims |
| **"Trend-scroller Tyler"** | 18–27 | Novelty, social proof, "TikTok made me buy it" | Price vs Temu, delivery speed |

## What these customers expect in 2026

- **Fast delivery:** 2–7 days is the norm set by Amazon/US warehouses. 2–4 week delivery drives refunds and chargebacks.
- **Social proof:** real reviews with photos/video, creator content.
- **Transparent pricing and returns:** a clear policy, no surprise duties.
- **Mobile-first checkout:** Shop Pay, Apple Pay, PayPal, buy-now-pay-later (Klarna/Afterpay) for $50+ orders.
- **Post-purchase communication:** tracking links, proactive delay emails.
