# 07 — Marketing

> Research date: November 2026. Ad benchmarks are mostly vendor-published (Triple Whale, Madgicx, AdCore, Metricool) and vary widely; our own account data replaces them as soon as we have it.

## Channel mix

| Channel | Role | 2026 benchmarks | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta (Facebook + Instagram) ads** | Main paid acquisition engine; broad targeting + Advantage+ shopping campaigns | CPM ~$14–15; ecommerce CPC ~$0.60–0.70 | ★★★ launch |
| **TikTok ads + Spark Ads** | Cheaper reach, younger audience, creative testing | CPM median ~$4 (Triple Whale DTC), typical $6–9; up to $9–14 in some reports | ★★★ launch |
| **TikTok Shop + affiliate creators** | In-app checkout, creators paid on commission | 10–20% creator commission typical | ★★ after US fulfillment proven |
| **UGC / influencer seeding** | Ad creative source + social proof | $100–300 per UGC video; gifting micro-influencers | ★★★ launch |
| **Email & SMS (Klaviyo/Omnisend)** | Retention, abandoned cart, post-purchase upsell; often 20–30% of revenue in mature stores | Low cost | ★★★ set up before launch |
| **Google Shopping / Performance Max** | Captures search demand once product is known | Higher intent, lower volume | ★★ month 2–3 |
| **Pinterest** | Home, decor, wellness niches | Lower CPM, slower | ★ optional |
| **SEO / content** | Long-term, brand-led stores only | Slow (6–12 mo) | ★ later |

DTC median CPA ~$17 (Triple Whale panel); CPA varies widely by vertical and offer, so use break-even ROAS (≈1.85 in our unit economics model) as the guardrail, not CPM.

## Conversion benchmarks (Shopify)

- Store conversion rate: median **~1.4%** (Littledata, 2,800 stores); top 20% ≥ 3.2%, top 10% ≥ 4.7%. A 2026 Shogun study: mean 2.6%, median 1.7%.
- Add-to-cart rate: ~4.6% average; top 20% ≥ 7.5%.
- Category: health & beauty converts higher (~3% top quartile) than electronics (~1.8%).

## Launch playbook (first 90 days)

1. **Pre-launch (weeks 0–2):** brand, 1–3 hero products sampled, product pages with video/GIFs, reviews imported only from genuine buyers, Klaviyo flows (welcome, abandoned checkout, browse abandonment, post-purchase, win-back), pixels + Conversions API installed.
2. **Creative testing (weeks 2–6):** 3–5 angles × 3 hooks each; $30–50/day per ad set on Meta, similar on TikTok. Kill ads under 1% CTR or above 1.5× target CPA; iterate on winners.
3. **Offer optimization:** bundles, quantity breaks, free-shipping threshold, guarantee; target AOV +20–30%.
4. **Scale (weeks 6–12):** increase budget 20–30% every 2–3 days on ads above target ROAS; add new creatives weekly (creative fatigue is the main limit); launch Google Shopping and TikTok Shop.
5. **Retention:** SMS opt-in at checkout, replenishment/accessory emails, loyalty discount for second order.

## Creative principles

- First 3 seconds: show the problem or the product working. Native, phone-shot UGC beats polished ads.
- Proven angles: problem → solution, before/after, unboxing, "3 reasons why", founder story, comparison vs. cheap alternative (quality, speed, guarantee).
- Every claim must be truthful and substantiated (see `08-risks.md` — FTC).

## Compliance in marketing

- **No fake reviews or bought testimonials** — FTC Consumer Review Rule allows civil penalties up to ~$53,088 per violation; FTC sent warning letters in late 2025.
- Influencers must disclose paid relationships (#ad).
- **Shipping-time promises must be realistic** (FTC Mail Order Rule).
- No fake scarcity or fake "was" prices; no health/medical claims without substantiation.
- Collect email/SMS consent properly (TCPA for SMS; CAN-SPAM; GDPR for EU visitors).
