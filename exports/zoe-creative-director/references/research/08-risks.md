# 08 — Biggest Risks

> Research date: November 2026. Ranked by likelihood × impact for a new Shopify dropshipping store.

## Context: most stores fail

Estimates vary by definition: **80–90% of dropshipping stores fail within the first year** (several industry roundups; Spocket data cited at ~90% within four months), and TrueProfit estimates only **1–5% consistently earn net profit** after all costs. Many "failures" are stores that were never seriously run, but the base rate is still harsh. The risks below are the usual causes.

## Risk register

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Unprofitable ad spend** — CPA higher than contribution margin; creative fatigue; rising CPMs | High | High | Know break-even ROAS before spending; daily kill rules; weekly new creatives; raise AOV; cap test budget |
| 2 | **Payment processor holds/reserves** — Shopify Payments, PayPal or Stripe hold 20–100% of payouts for up to 120 days, often triggered by sales spikes, long delivery times or chargebacks | Medium–High | High (cash flow) | Fast US-warehouse shipping; upload tracking promptly; clear policies; scale spend gradually; keep 2–3 months of cash; keep supplier invoices and tracking as evidence; have a backup gateway |
| 3 | **Chargebacks and refunds** — "item not received", "not as described" | High | Medium–High | Realistic delivery promises, proactive tracking emails, quality samples, responsive support, chargeback alerts (e.g. Shopify Protect, Chargeflow). Keep chargeback rate < 0.5% |
| 4 | **Supplier failure** — stockouts, quality drift, slow processing, wrong items | High | Medium | 2 suppliers per hero SKU; order periodic test purchases; inventory-sync app; written SLA with agent/supplier |
| 5 | **Tariffs and trade-policy changes** — de minimis ended 2025; tariff rates on China-origin goods change often | High | Medium–High | Prefer US-warehoused stock; price with a duty buffer; diversify origin countries; monitor CBP/USTR notices |
| 6 | **Regulatory / legal** | Medium | High | See below |
| 7 | **Price undercutting & copycats** — Temu/Amazon sell the same item for less; competitors clone our ads | High | Medium | Brand, bundles, exclusive variants or private label, content moat, customer service, email list |
| 8 | **Ad account bans** — Meta/TikTok disable accounts for policy breaches or poor feedback scores | Medium | High | Follow ad policies; avoid before/after health claims; maintain good Meta feedback score; backup business manager/ad accounts |
| 9 | **Product liability & safety** — injuries from defective goods; CPSC rules for kids' products; batteries | Low–Medium | High | Avoid risky categories; obtain supplier certifications (CPC, FCC, CE); general liability/product insurance |
| 10 | **IP infringement** — selling knockoffs, using trademarked names or stolen images | Medium | High | Own photos/videos; no branded knockoffs; trademark search on our brand name |
| 11 | **Founder burnout / key-person risk** | Medium | Medium | Document SOPs; automate with agents and VAs |

## Regulatory checklist (US focus)

- **FTC Mail, Internet or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule:** must have a reasonable basis for advertised shipping times (or ship within 30 days); must get consent to delays or refund. Fashion Nova paid $9.3M for violations. Penalties are tens of thousands of dollars per violation (inflation-adjusted annually).
- **FTC Consumer Review Rule (2024):** bans fake reviews, buying reviews, suppressing negative reviews, undisclosed insider reviews. Up to ~$53,088 per violation; warning letters issued late 2025.
- **FTC Endorsement Guides:** influencers must disclose material connections.
- **Health/cosmetic claims:** FTC substantiation; FDA rules for anything ingested or presented as treating a condition.
- **CPSC:** children's products need a Children's Product Certificate and third-party testing.
- **Sales tax:** economic nexus in each state; Shopify Tax helps.
- **Privacy:** CCPA/CPRA (California) and other state laws; GDPR/UK GDPR if selling to Europe; cookie consent.
- **SMS/email:** TCPA consent for SMS; CAN-SPAM for email.
- **Accessibility:** ADA website-accessibility lawsuits target ecommerce stores; use accessible themes and alt text.

## Early-warning metrics to monitor

| Metric | Healthy | Act now |
|---|---|---|
| ROAS vs break-even | > 1.3× break-even | < break-even for 3+ days |
| Chargeback rate | < 0.3% | > 0.65% (card network monitoring thresholds start near 0.9–1%) |
| Refund rate | < 5% | > 10% |
| Order-to-delivery time | ≤ 7 days | > 10 days |
| "Where is my order" tickets | < 10% of orders | > 20% |
| Payout reserve | none | any new reserve notice |
