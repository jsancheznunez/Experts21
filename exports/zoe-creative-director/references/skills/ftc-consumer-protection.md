# FTC advertising & consumer-protection rules

Source: `references/research/08-risks.md`, `references/research/07-marketing.md`. Not legal advice — escalate unclear cases to e-commerce compliance counsel.

## Mail, Internet or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule
- Must have a **reasonable basis** to ship within the advertised time; if no time stated, within **30 days**.
- If you can't meet it: notify the buyer, get **consent to the delay or refund** promptly.
- Penalties: tens of thousands of dollars per violation (inflation-adjusted yearly). Fashion Nova paid **$9.3M** (2020).

## Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule (2024)
Prohibits: fake or AI-generated reviews attributed to non-existent customers; buying reviews conditioned on sentiment; insider/employee reviews without disclosure; suppressing negative reviews (filtering, intimidation); company-controlled "independent" review sites; fake social-media indicators (bought followers/views). Civil penalties up to **~$53,088 per violation** (2025 figure; check 2026 adjustment). FTC sent warning letters in late 2025.
**Allowed:** asking all customers for honest reviews; incentives if not conditioned on positive sentiment and disclosed; importing genuine reviews with clear source (no copying supplier/AliExpress reviews as our own).

## Endorsement Guides
Creators must clearly disclose material connections (#ad, "paid partnership" tool) in the content itself; we must monitor compliance and not script false claims.

## Advertising claims
- Objective claims need **competent and reliable evidence** before publishing; health claims need competent and reliable scientific evidence.
- No disease/treatment claims for wellness products (also FDA territory).
- **Former price comparisons** must reflect real prior prices offered in good faith.
- **Fake scarcity/urgency** (resetting timers, false stock counts) is deceptive.
- "Free" must be genuinely free with conditions clear; "Made in USA" must be "all or virtually all".
- Negative option/subscriptions: clear terms, express consent, easy cancellation.
