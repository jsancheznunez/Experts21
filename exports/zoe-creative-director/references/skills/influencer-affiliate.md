# Influencer & affiliate management

Context: 56% of US Gen Z have bought because of an influencer; TikTok Shop US ≈ $23.4B in 2026 (`references/research/03-target-customers.md`, `references/research/04-competitors.md`).

## Program types
| Type | Pay model | Use |
|---|---|---|
| UGC creators | Flat fee per video (~$100–300) | Ad creative; no posting on their channel needed |
| Gifted seeding | Free product | Micro-influencers (5k–100k) for organic content and reviews |
| Paid posts | Flat fee (+ usage rights) | Proven creators in the niche |
| Affiliates | Commission (typ. 10–20%) | Scalable, pay for results |
| TikTok Shop affiliates | Platform-managed commission (open or targeted plans) | Creator-driven sales in-app |

## Recruiting
Find creators already making content in the niche (TikTok search, Creator Marketplace/TikTok One, Instagram hashtags, our own customers). Check: engagement quality (real comments), audience country, brand safety, past disclosures, content style fit.

## Outreach & terms
Short personal message → product + offer → clear deliverables, dates, usage rights, disclosure requirement, payment terms. Use a written agreement (see contracts-entity-ip).

## Brief essentials
Key benefit, persona, must-show moments, talking points (own words), **do-not-say list** (no unsubstantiated or medical claims), disclosure method, links/codes with UTMs.

## Compliance
Disclosure in the content itself (#ad, platform paid-partnership label); we monitor and require fixes; no fake reviews by creators; no buying followers/views (FTC Consumer Review Rule).

## Tracking
Unique codes and UTM links per creator; track views, clicks, orders, revenue, CPA, content reused in ads.

## Output
Creator shortlist with fit scores, outreach templates, offer/commission structure, brief, and tracking sheet.
