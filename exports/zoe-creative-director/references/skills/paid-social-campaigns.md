# Paid social campaign management

Benchmarks (`references/research/07-marketing.md`, vendor-grade, use own data ASAP): Meta CPM ~$14–15, ecommerce CPC ~$0.60–0.70; TikTok CPM median ~$4 (typical $6–9); DTC median CPA ~$17.

## Prerequisites
Tracking verified (tracking-attribution) · break-even ROAS and target CPA known (unit-economics) · 6–15 creatives ready (ugc-creative-production) · business manager with verified domain and backup admin · payment method with enough limit.

## Launch structure
**Meta**
- Testing: 1 campaign, ABO or CBO, broad targeting (age/country only), 3–5 ad sets by angle, 3 ads each; $30–50/day per ad set; optimize for Purchase.
- Scaling: Advantage+ Shopping campaign with proven creatives; exclude/limit existing customers as configured.
**TikTok**
- Smart+ / web conversion campaign, broad targeting, 3–5 ad groups by angle; Spark Ads with creator posts (with permission).
- Minimum ~$50/day per ad group for learning.

## Kill / iterate / scale rules
- Kill an ad at **spend ≥ 1.5× target CPA with 0 purchases**, or CTR < 1% after ~2,000 impressions.
- Keep in testing while CPA ≤ break-even CPA.
- Scale winners: raise budget **20–30% every 2–3 days** while ROAS ≥ target; duplicate into scaling campaign.
- Refresh creative when frequency > ~2.5–3 (prospecting) and CPA drifts 20%+ above target.
- Judge on 3–7 day windows, not hourly swings; use blended MER weekly.

## Account health
Follow ad policies (no before/after body claims, no personal-attribute targeting language like "Are you overweight?", no misleading claims); keep Meta feedback score healthy (shipping speed and accurate ads matter); don't spike budgets abruptly (also triggers payment holds, see chargeback-management); keep backup ad accounts and admins.

## Output
Media plan (budget by platform/stage), campaign structure, rule set, daily/weekly review template, and budget changes framed as recommendations for human approval.
