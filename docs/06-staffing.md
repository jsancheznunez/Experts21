# 06 — Staffing

> Research date: November 2026. A dropshipping store has no warehouse, so staffing is about **marketing, creative, customer service and operations**. Most functions can be done by the founder plus freelancers and AI agents at first.

## Functions the business needs

| Function | What it involves | Stage 1 (launch, <$10k/mo) | Stage 2 ($10–50k/mo) | Stage 3 ($50k+/mo) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Founder / GM** | Strategy, product selection, supplier relationships, finances | Founder | Founder | Founder |
| **Product research** | Trend scanning, ad-library spying, sampling | Founder + AI agent | AI agent + VA | Product manager |
| **Store build & CRO** | Theme, product pages, copy, A/B tests | Founder + AI agent | Freelance Shopify dev (one-off) | Part-time CRO specialist |
| **Paid ads (media buying)** | Meta/TikTok/Google campaigns, testing, scaling | Founder | Freelance media buyer | Full-time buyer or agency |
| **Creative / UGC** | Short videos, images, ad copy | Founder + AI tools + UGC creators per video | 2–5 UGC creators monthly | In-house editor + creator roster |
| **Email/SMS marketing** | Klaviyo flows, campaigns | AI agent + templates | Freelancer | Retention marketer |
| **Customer service** | Email/chat, "where is my order", returns, disputes | Founder + AI chatbot | Part-time VA (4h/day) | 1–2 full-time VAs covering US hours |
| **Order operations** | Supplier orders, tracking, exceptions, supplier QC | Automation app (DSers/AutoDS) | VA | Ops manager |
| **Bookkeeping & tax** | Reconciliation, sales tax, profit tracking | Profit app + accountant at year end | Monthly bookkeeper | Bookkeeper + CPA |
| **Legal/compliance** | Policies, claims review, trademark | Templates | Lawyer review on demand | Retained counsel |

## How AI agents fit (for the next build phase)

The "expert agents" team can cover research and drafting for most of these roles. Humans should still own:
- **Approval of spend** (ad budgets, inventory, supplier payments)
- **Health/safety claims and legal policies** (regulatory exposure)
- **Chargeback and dispute responses** (evidence, judgment)
- **Supplier relationships** (negotiation, quality escalation)

Suggested agent roles that map to the functions above: Market Research, Product Hunter, Brand & Store Builder, Copywriter/Creative Director, Media Buyer/Ad Analyst, Email/Retention, Customer Support, Operations/Supplier Manager, Finance/Unit Economics, and Compliance/Risk.
