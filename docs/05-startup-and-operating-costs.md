# 05 — Startup and Operating Costs

> Research date: November 2026. USD. Verify live prices on vendor pages before committing; Shopify and app pricing change frequently.

## Shopify plans (2026)

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing (per mo) | Online card rate (Shopify Payments) | Extra fee if using 3rd-party gateway |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $5 | — | 5% | n/a (social selling only) |
| **Basic** | $39 | $29 | ~2.9% + $0.30 | 2% |
| **Grow** | $105 | $79 | ~2.7% + $0.30 | 1% |
| Advanced | $399 | $299 | ~2.5% + $0.30 | 0.6% |
| Plus | from ~$2,300 | — | negotiated | — |

Start on **Basic**; move to **Grow** when monthly revenue reaches ~$10–15k (the lower card rate pays for the plan difference). PayPal adds ~3.5% on its share of orders.

## Variable costs per order (unit economics)

Example: $45 retail hero product, US-warehouse fulfillment.

| Line | $ | % of revenue |
|---|---|---|
| Product cost (incl. duties via US warehouse) | 11.00 | 24% |
| Shipping to customer | 5.00 | 11% |
| Payment processing (2.9% + $0.30) | 1.61 | 4% |
| Refunds/returns/chargebacks reserve | 2.25 | 5% |
| App costs allocated | 1.00 | 2% |
| **Contribution before ads** | **24.14** | **54%** |
| Customer acquisition cost (paid ads) | 15–20 | 33–44% |
| **Net profit per order** | **~4–9** | **~10–20%** |

Key ratios for the agent team:
- **Break-even ROAS** = 1 ÷ contribution margin ≈ 1 ÷ 0.54 ≈ **1.85**. Target ad ROAS of **2.5–3.0+**.
- Raise **average order value** (bundles, "buy 2 get 10% off", post-purchase upsells) to lift margin.
- Industry benchmarks: net margins 15–20% for experienced sellers, under 10% for beginners; electronics 10–15%.

## Cash-flow notes

- Ad platforms charge daily; suppliers are paid at order time; Shopify payouts arrive in 2–3 business days, **but new dropshipping stores can get payout reserves (holds) of weeks to 120 days** (see `08-risks.md`). Keep **2–3 months of ad spend plus fixed costs** in working capital.
- Sales tax: Shopify Tax calculates US sales tax; register in states where economic nexus thresholds are crossed (often $100k sales).
