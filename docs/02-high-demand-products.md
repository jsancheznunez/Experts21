# 02 — High-Demand Products

> Research date: November 2026. Most "trending product" lists are published by suppliers (CJdropshipping, Printify, Syncee, Dropified) who benefit from you selling their catalog. Treat them as idea lists, then validate demand ourselves.

## Categories that recur across 2026 lists

| Category | Examples | Why it works | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pet supplies** | No-pull harnesses, collars, leashes, slow feeders, lick mats, grooming gloves, orthopedic beds | Emotional purchase, repeat buyers, strong video content | Safety claims; sizing returns |
| **Home & kitchen** | Organizers, gadget-style kitchen tools, LED/ambient lighting, "emotional-support" decor | Broad audience, visual demos | Bulky items = high shipping cost |
| **Wellness & sleep** | Sleep masks, mouth tape, posture correctors, massage tools, at-home wellness kits | High perceived value, consumable re-orders | Health claims are regulated (FTC/FDA) |
| **Smart/portable gadgets** | Portable blenders, phone accessories, mini projectors, car accessories | Viral demo potential | Electronics: thin margins (10–15%), defect returns, battery shipping restrictions |
| **Beauty tools** | Facial rollers, LED masks, hair tools, nail kits | High markup, influencer-friendly | Skin/health claims; cosmetics rules |
| **Eco-friendly / customizable** | Reusable household goods, print-on-demand apparel and gifts | Differentiation, less price comparison | POD has lower margins; slower production |
| **Athleisure & fitness** | Resistance bands, yoga gear, gym accessories | Lifestyle branding, bundles | Apparel sizing returns |

## Selection criteria (score each candidate 1–5)

1. **Visible demand** — Google Trends rising or stable 12+ months, TikTok/Meta Ad Library shows several advertisers running it for weeks (proof it converts).
2. **"Wow" or problem-solving in 3 seconds** — can be shown on a short video.
3. **Price point $25–$80 retail** — high enough to absorb ~$15–35 customer acquisition cost, low enough for impulse buying.
4. **Landed cost ≤ 30–35% of retail** (product + shipping + duties) so gross margin is ~65%+.
5. **Hard to price-check** — not the same listing that appears on Temu/Amazon for $6. Bundles, custom branding, or exclusive variants help.
6. **US/EU warehouse stock available** — 2–7 day delivery.
7. **Low return/defect risk** — small, light, non-fragile, no sizing complexity, no batteries if possible.
8. **Repeat potential** — consumables or accessories that allow a second sale.
9. **Low regulatory risk** — avoid ingestibles, medical claims, children's products needing CPSC certification, knives/weapons, trademarked or knock-off items.

## Recommended starting direction

A **single-niche brand** (e.g. pets, home organization, or sleep/wellness) with 10–30 SKUs, built around 1–3 "hero" products plus accessories for order-value upsells. Pet and home categories score best on the criteria above.

## Validation tools

- Google Trends, Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center (top ads / trending products)
- Amazon Best Sellers & Movers and Shakers; TikTok Shop best-seller lists
- Product research tools: Minea, Sell The Trend, Kalodata, AutoDS product finder
- Order samples before listing — check quality, packaging, and real delivery time.
