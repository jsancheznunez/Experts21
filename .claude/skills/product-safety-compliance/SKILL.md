# Product safety & regulatory screening

Not legal advice — escalate uncertain items to e-commerce compliance counsel.

## Risk tiers
| Tier | Categories | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Red — avoid** | Ingestibles/supplements, medical devices or treatment claims, children's toys/products for ≤ 12 without certified testing, car seats/cribs, weapons/knives, vapes, counterfeit or branded look-alikes, hoverboard-type lithium products | Do not list |
| **Amber — documents required** | Electronics (FCC), lithium batteries (UN38.3 test summary, shipping restrictions), cosmetics/skin contact (FDA MoCRA: registration/listing, safety substantiation, labeling), textiles (care/fiber labels), products touching food (food-contact compliance), pet products with health claims | List only with documents on file |
| **Green** | Simple home organizers, non-electronic pet accessories, décor, fitness accessories without claims | Standard QC |

## Required evidence (as applicable)
- **Children's Product Certificate (CPC)** from a CPSC-accepted lab for products primarily for children 12 and under; tracking labels.
- **General Certificate of Conformity (GCC)** for adult products under certain CPSC rules.
- **FCC** Supplier's Declaration of Conformity or ID for electronics.
- **UN38.3** and SDS for lithium batteries.
- **Prop 65** warning if the product contains listed chemicals sold into California.
- Country-of-origin marking; correct HS code.

## Screening steps
1. Classify the product tier.
2. Check CPSC recalls (cpsc.gov/Recalls) and Amazon/marketplace restrictions for the category.
3. Request certificates from the supplier; verify lab names.
4. Check IP: brand names, patented designs, copyrighted images; search USPTO and Google Patents for distinctive designs.
5. Review planned marketing claims (see ftc-consumer-protection).
6. Record decision and documents in the product file.

## Output
Tier, required documents and status, IP check result, claim restrictions, and a go/hold/reject decision.
