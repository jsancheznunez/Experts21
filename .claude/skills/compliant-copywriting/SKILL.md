# Persuasive & compliant copywriting

## Product page structure
1. **Headline:** the outcome, not the feature ("Walks without pulling — from day one").
2. **Sub-head:** who it's for + key proof (rating, number of customers, guarantee).
3. **Hero media:** demo video/GIF in the first screen.
4. **3–5 benefit bullets:** benefit first, feature second.
5. **Social proof:** genuine reviews with photos.
6. **Offer block:** price, bundle/quantity breaks, shipping promise, guarantee.
7. **How it works / what's included / specs / sizing.**
8. **FAQ:** answer the top objections from customer-personas.
9. **Shipping & returns summary** with links to policies.

## Frameworks
- Ads: Hook (≤ 3 s / first line) → Problem → Solution → Proof → Offer → CTA.
- PAS (Problem–Agitate–Solve) and Before–After–Bridge for short copy.
- Use voice-of-customer phrases from reviews and comments.

## Compliance rules (non-negotiable)
- Every objective claim ("reduces snoring", "lasts 2× longer") needs evidence on file. No disease/treatment claims for wellness products.
- No fake urgency (countdown timers that reset, false "only 3 left").
- Compare-at prices must be real (see pricing-margin-modeling).
- Shipping times stated must match reality (FTC Mail Order Rule).
- Reviews and testimonials must be genuine and unedited in meaning; disclose incentives (FTC Consumer Review Rule; see ftc-consumer-protection).
- "Free", "guarantee" and "made in" claims must be accurate with conditions disclosed.
- Flag any claim you are not sure about for compliance review instead of softening it silently.

## Tone
Match the brand voice guide (brand-positioning). Mobile-first: short paragraphs, scannable bullets, no walls of text.

## Output
Copy in final form, with variants for testing where useful, and a list of claims that need substantiation or legal review.
