# Google Shopping, feeds & SEO

Priority: month 2–3 after paid social proves the product (`docs/07-marketing.md`).

## Merchant Center & feed
- Connect via the Shopify Google & YouTube app; verify and claim the domain.
- Feed quality: descriptive titles (Brand + product type + key attribute + size/color), GTIN or `identifier_exists = no` for unbranded goods, accurate price and availability, high-quality images on white/clean background, product category, shipping and return settings matching the policies.
- Common disapprovals: misrepresentation (missing policies/contact info, unrealistic claims), price mismatch, shipping time mismatch, policy-restricted products. Fix root causes, don't just resubmit.
- Free listings on by default.

## Campaigns
- Start: Standard Shopping or Performance Max limited to hero products, target ROAS set from break-even ROAS × 1.3 after ~30 conversions.
- Branded search campaign once brand searches appear (cheap, protects the brand).
- Negative keywords for irrelevant/free/DIY searches; exclude Temu/AliExpress-style queries if they don't convert.

## On-site SEO (long-term, brand-led stores)
- Unique product descriptions (never the supplier's copied text), title tags ≤ 60 chars, meta descriptions, alt text, clean URLs.
- Collection pages with helpful intro copy targeting category keywords.
- Structured data (Product, Offer, Review) via theme/app; valid ratings only from genuine reviews.
- Content: buying guides and how-tos for the niche; internal links to collections.
- Technical: fast mobile pages, no duplicate content from variants/tags, sitemap submitted in Search Console.

## Output
Feed audit with fixes, campaign plan with budgets and targets, keyword/content plan, and Search Console baseline.
