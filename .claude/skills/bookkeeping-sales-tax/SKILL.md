# Bookkeeping & sales-tax compliance

## Setup
- Separate business bank account and card; accounting software (QuickBooks Online or Xero) with a Shopify connector (A2X, Synder) that books **payouts by component**, not as single deposits.
- Accrual-style reporting so ad spend and COGS match the revenue month.

## Chart of accounts (minimum)
Revenue: product sales, shipping income, discounts (contra), refunds (contra).
COGS: product cost, duties/tariffs, supplier shipping, packaging.
Expenses: payment processing, Shopify plan, apps, ad spend (per platform), creative/UGC, contractors/VAs, chargeback fees and losses, software, professional fees.
Liabilities: sales tax payable, payout reserves (held funds) as a receivable.

## Month-end close
1. Reconcile Shopify payouts to bank (fees, refunds, reserves, chargebacks).
2. Match supplier invoices to orders (COGS per order).
3. Reconcile ad platform invoices.
4. Review gross margin and contribution vs the unit-economics model.

## Sales tax (US)
- **Economic nexus:** most states use $100k in sales (some also 200 transactions) in the current or prior year. Track per state monthly.
- Physical nexus can arise from inventory in a state — check whether supplier warehouses create obligations for the merchant (usually not when the supplier owns the inventory, but confirm with a CPA).
- Register before collecting; enable collection in Shopify Tax per registered state; file on each state's schedule.
- Marketplace facilitators (TikTok Shop, Amazon) collect tax on their own sales.

## Income tax & entity
Track estimated quarterly taxes; entity choice and elections with a CPA (see contracts-entity-ip). 1099-NEC for US contractors ≥ $600.

## Rules
Not legal or tax advice: flag decisions for a licensed CPA. Never mix personal and business spending.

## Output
Setup checklist, chart of accounts, close checklist, nexus tracker by state, and questions for the CPA.
