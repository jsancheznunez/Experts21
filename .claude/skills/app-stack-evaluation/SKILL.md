# App stack evaluation

## Reference stack (docs/04, docs/05)
| Job | Options (verify 2026 pricing on vendor pages) | Typical cost |
|---|---|---|
| Sourcing / order automation | DSers, AutoDS, CJdropshipping, Zendrop, Spocket, Doba, TopDawg, Syncee | $0–80/mo |
| Reviews | Judge.me, Loox, Okendo | $15–35/mo |
| Email / SMS | Klaviyo, Omnisend, Shopify Email | $0–150/mo (list size) |
| Upsells / bundles | Native bundles, ReConvert, Zipify, Kaching | $10–50/mo |
| Helpdesk / chat | Shopify Inbox, Gorgias, Tidio | $0–60/mo |
| Profit tracking | TrueProfit, BeProfit | $0–50/mo |
| Chargeback protection | Shopify Protect, Chargeflow, Signifyd | % of orders/recovered |

## Evaluation criteria (score 1–5)
1. **Fit:** does it solve our exact job without workarounds?
2. **Total cost:** subscription + usage fees + % of revenue at 3 revenue levels.
3. **Performance impact:** script weight, page-speed change (test before/after).
4. **Data & permissions:** scopes requested, PII handling, GDPR/CCPA posture.
5. **Integration:** works with our other tools (Klaviyo, helpdesk, supplier app).
6. **Vendor risk:** reviews count and recency, support quality, lock-in, export options.
7. **Built for Shopify** badge / native alternative available?

## Rules
- One app per job; remove overlaps. Every app must justify its cost in orders or hours saved.
- Prefer native Shopify features first.
- Trial before paying; uninstall cleanly (remove leftover theme code).
- Comparison sites and vendor blogs conflict on pricing; confirm on the vendor's own pricing page and date it.

## Output
A comparison table, a recommended stack with monthly cost at launch and at $20k/month revenue, and the install/removal plan.
