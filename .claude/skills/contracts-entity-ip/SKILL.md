# Contracts, entity & IP

Not legal advice — produce issue lists and drafts for a licensed attorney to review.

## Entity
- Typical: **US LLC** (state filing $50–300, plus registered agent; $300–800 all-in recommended) + EIN; consider S-corp election with a CPA once profits justify it.
- Separate bank account, operating agreement, and business insurance (general/product liability).
- Foreign founders: US LLC is possible; banking, ITIN/EIN and tax treaty issues need a specialist.

## Contracts checklist
| Agreement | Must cover |
|---|---|
| Supplier/agent | Pricing, processing and delivery SLAs, quality standards, defect/lost-parcel remedies, certifications, IP warranties and indemnity, confidentiality, no direct solicitation of our customers, termination |
| UGC creator | Deliverables, payment, **usage rights** (paid ads, duration, territories, whitelisting/Spark Ads), revisions, FTC disclosure duty, no false claims, work-for-hire/license, morality clause |
| Affiliate/influencer | Commission, cookie window, disclosure, brand guidelines, prohibited claims, payment terms, termination |
| Contractor/VA | Scope, rates, confidentiality, data protection, IP assignment, access revocation, independent-contractor status |
| Website terms | Limitation of liability, governing law, dispute resolution, acceptable use |

## Trademark & IP
- **Clearance before adopting a brand:** USPTO search (exact and similar marks in relevant classes, e.g. 18, 21, 28, 35), state registrations, common-law use (web, Amazon, social).
- File a US trademark application once the name is cleared (consider an intent-to-use basis).
- Never use others' trademarks in product names, ads or keywords in a way that implies affiliation; no "dupe" marketing of branded goods.
- Use only images and videos we own or have licensed; supplier photos may be copyrighted by brands.
- Respond to IP complaints/DMCA notices promptly; remove listings while assessing.

## Output
Entity recommendation with costs, contract issue lists or draft terms, trademark clearance findings, and open questions for an attorney.
