# Returns, refunds & dispute handling

## Policy design (dropshipping-specific)
- **Window:** 30 days from delivery is the norm; a guarantee can raise conversion.
- **Return address:** a domestic returns address (supplier US warehouse or a returns service). Never require customers to ship to China.
- **Low-value items:** "returnless refund" or replacement is often cheaper than return shipping.
- **Defective/wrong/damaged:** replacement or full refund, no return needed; photo evidence.
- **Change of mind:** customer pays return shipping (state clearly) or offer store credit.
- **Exclusions:** hygiene items once opened, custom products — must be stated before purchase.
- Policy must match what agents actually do, and be linked in footer, product page and checkout.

## Resolution ladder (cheapest path that keeps the customer)
1. Help (usage tips, tracking update).
2. Partial refund or discount on next order.
3. Replacement.
4. Full refund (no return for low value).
5. Return and refund.

**Refund fast when the claim is plausible**: a $30 refund costs far less than a chargeback (fee + lost goods + risk to payment account).

## Dispute prevention
- Respond within 24 h; proactive delay notices.
- Clear statement descriptor (brand name).
- Never ignore "I'll contact my bank" messages — escalate to priority.

## Metrics
Refund rate < 5% (act > 10%), return reasons by product and supplier, time to resolution.

## Output
Policy text (for legal review), resolution rules for support agents, and a root-cause report linking refunds to products/suppliers.
