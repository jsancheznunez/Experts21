# Creative briefing & UGC production

Context: creative fatigue is the main limit on scaling paid social (`docs/07-marketing.md`). Native, phone-shot UGC usually beats polished ads.

## Angle library (start here)
Problem → solution · Before/after (only if truthful) · Unboxing/first impression · "3 reasons why" · Founder story · Comparison vs cheap alternative (quality, speed, guarantee) · Day-in-the-life · Expert/vet/pro tip · Reaction/stitch of a viral video.

## Hook rules (first 3 seconds)
- Show the product working or the problem happening; no logo intros.
- On-screen text that states the outcome or calls out the persona ("Dog owners, stop doing this").
- Make 3 hooks per body: the hook drives most of the performance difference.

## Creator brief template
```
Product + key benefit:
Persona (from customer-personas):
Angle:
Must-show shots: (1) hook … (2) demo … (3) proof … (4) offer/CTA
Talking points (in creator's own words, no scripts read verbatim):
Do NOT say: (unsubstantiated claims, competitor names, medical claims)
Format: 9:16, 15–45 s, natural light, phone camera, captions
Deliverables: raw clips + 1 edit, 3 hook variations
Usage rights: paid ads, X months, whitelisting/Spark Ads permission
Disclosure: #ad / paid partnership where posted organically
```

## Testing pipeline
- Weekly: 3–5 new concepts × 3 hooks.
- Judge on: thumb-stop rate (3-s views ÷ impressions), hold rate, CTR, CPA vs target.
- Iterate winners (new hooks, new creators, new first frame); retire ads when frequency rises and CPA drifts above target.
- Keep a creative log: concept, angle, hook, creator, spend, CPA, ROAS, learnings.

## Costs (2026)
UGC video ~$100–300 each; gifting micro-influencers for organic content.

## Output
Briefs ready to send, a shot list, hook variants, and the testing plan with kill/iterate rules.
