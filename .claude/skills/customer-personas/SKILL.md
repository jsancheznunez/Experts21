# Persona & customer journey mapping

Baseline: `docs/03-target-customers.md`.

## Audience segments (2026)
| Segment | Channel | Traits |
|---|---|---|
| Millennials (30–45) — primary | Meta first, TikTok second | Highest spend; often parents and pet owners; ~43% bought in-app recently |
| Gen Z (18–29) — secondary | TikTok, Reels, creators | Influencer-driven (56% bought because of one); most price-sensitive; compare with Temu/Shein |
| Gen X / Boomers (45+) — tertiary | Facebook feed, email | Higher trust once converted, fewer price comparisons |

## Starter personas
| Persona | Motivation | Objections to answer |
|---|---|---|
| Pet parent Priya (28–42) | Pet wellbeing, cute content, gifting | Quality, safety, shipping time |
| Busy-home Brian (32–50) | Saves time, tidies home | Does it really work? Returns |
| Wellness Wendy (25–45) | Sleep, stress, self-care | Proof, honest claims |
| Trend-scroller Tyler (18–27) | Novelty, social proof | Price vs Temu, delivery speed |

## Journey stages
Discover (ad/creator) → Consider (product page, reviews, price check) → Purchase (mobile checkout, Shop Pay/BNPL) → Wait (tracking emails, 2–7 days expected) → Use (unboxing, how-to) → Repeat/refer (accessory email, review request, loyalty).

At each stage list: the persona's question, the asset that answers it, and the metric that shows it works.

## Rules
- Build personas from evidence (reviews, comments, tickets, surveys), not stereotypes; mark assumptions.
- Use voice-of-customer language verbatim in copy and creative.
