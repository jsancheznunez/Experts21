# Trend & demand validation

## Signals to collect (aim for at least 4)
| Signal | Where | Strong evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Search interest | Google Trends (12 months and 5 years, US) | Rising or stable; not a single spike that has already decayed |
| Ad longevity | Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center "Top ads" | Several advertisers running ads for the product for 3+ weeks (ads that keep running are profitable) |
| Marketplace sales | Amazon Best Sellers / Movers & Shakers, TikTok Shop best sellers, Etsy | Product or close variants ranked in the category |
| Social engagement | TikTok/Instagram hashtag views, organic videos | Recent (last 60 days) organic videos with real comments asking "where to buy" |
| Research tools | Minea, Kalodata, Sell The Trend, AutoDS finder | Sales estimates rising, multiple stores selling |
| Seasonality | Google Trends 5-year view | Know the peak months; avoid launching right after a peak |

## Classify the trend
- **Evergreen:** steady multi-year demand (pet harnesses, kitchen organizers). Best for a brand.
- **Growing trend:** rising 6+ months across several signals. Good entry window.
- **Fad/spike:** one viral moment, sharp decay. Avoid unless the store is built for fast product rotation.
- **Saturated:** many advertisers, Temu/Amazon listings at a fraction of our price. Only enter with a clear differentiator.

## Red flags
- Demand only on one platform, or only from one creator's video.
- Ads are all from the same 1–2 stores (may be a test, not proof).
- Search interest falling for 3+ months.
- Most evidence comes from a supplier's "trending products" blog (vendor bias; see web-research).

## Output
A verdict (`validated`, `promising - test`, `weak`, `reject`), the trend class, the evidence table with dates and links, and seasonality notes.
